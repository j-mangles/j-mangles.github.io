# Grade 7/8 Classroom Video Audit — November 12, 2026

**Purpose:** identify a small, high-quality set of videos that can genuinely teach or deepen the current classroom lessons. This is a curation pass, not a “one link per lesson” exercise.

**Scope for this pass:** active Cells and Systems, the next Matter/Density unit, Grade 7 Fractions/Decimals/Percents and Probability, current Short Stories, the Think Lab's online source-evaluation work, and the 13-lesson Ancient Greece and 13-lesson Ancient Rome sequences being prepared on this branch.

**Review standard:** credible educator/institution and inspectable lesson description or transcript; clear match to a lesson objective; sensible Grade 7/8 use; short enough to excerpt where possible; explicit notes for complexity, simplification, violence, or missing perspectives. “Recommended” means worth a teacher preview and lesson use; it does not mean every video is ready to show without context.

## Quality calls and next actions

- **Best immediate classroom tests:** the selected Systems chapters, the cell tour excerpt, the Grade 7 percent visual, and the TED-Ed Athens democracy clip. These have the clearest match to active lessons.
- **Best history clips:** TED-Ed's democracy, Athenian daily life, Alexander, Roman soldier and Caesar selections; Khan Academy's Peloponnesian War and Rome overview/reconstruction. They have identifiable educators, strong explanatory structure, or transparent visual reconstruction.
- **Use with a frame:** fast overview videos, Khan Academy's broad history lectures, Sparta/war clips, the upper-status Roman sisters, Spartacus, and “Fall of Rome.” Each is useful for a narrow purpose but can leave out major perspectives or over-compress the history.
- **No-link is a valid audit result.** For early Aegean history, myth/religion, the Greek thinkers, the Punic Wars, and Roman Christianity, this pass found no video that clearly beats the existing map, source, discussion or interactive work. Do not add weak links just to fill a lesson.
- **ELA film texts:** this report includes one explicit writing mini-lesson, not a slate of short films. A new film-text shortlist should be screened separately for full content, sensitive themes, reliable classroom access and whether it offers a different learning opportunity from already-used “Alike,” “The Present” and “Hair Love.”
- **Site integration:** keep the audit as a teacher-facing resource first. Once a teacher selects specific clips, link only those from the matching lessons with a one-line viewing purpose; do not embed autoplay or add a video to every lesson.
- **Remaining project work:** complete the physical-device/iPad and smartboard review; teacher review and classroom pilot; finish the accessibility/interaction sweep; then continue the backlog's reusable ELA organizer and source-based Social Studies inquiry. The draft PR remains unmerged pending that review.
