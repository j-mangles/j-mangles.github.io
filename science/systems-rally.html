Body Systems Rally

Review game · Grade 8 Science · Four rounds · 8 points

A volleyball player ate lunch an hour ago. During a long rally, how do glucose and oxygen reach the same working muscle?

Work in pairs. Write an answer before the class reveals the evidence. Each round is worth two points. Switch speakers after each round.

Repair the fuel route

A teammate draws this route for glucose from the player’s sandwich.

  1. Stomach
  2. Leg muscle cell

On your board Draw a corrected route. Mark where glucose crosses a lining and circle what carries it to the muscle.

Score the route

  1. Show glucose crossing the small-intestine lining into a capillary.
  2. Show blood carrying the absorbed glucose to the working muscle cell.

Follow the oxygen

The player breathes in before jumping. Two arrows are missing their directions.

At the lungs

Air in a lung air sac   /   Blood in a capillary

At the muscle

Leg muscle cell   /   Blood in a capillary

On your board Draw the direction oxygen moves at each location. Explain how oxygen gets between the two locations.

Score the exchanges

  1. Oxygen crosses from air in the lungs into the blood.
  2. Blood carries oxygen to muscle capillaries, where it moves from the blood into muscle cells.

Read the recovery

At minute 0 play stops. Before playing, the athlete’s resting rate was 76 beats/min.

On your board Choose a minute after play stops. Use its reading and the resting rate to explain whether the heart rate is recovering.

Score the evidence

  1. Cite a matching minute and heart rate from the graph.
  2. Explain the trend toward rest using the comparison. For example, minute 4 is 92 beats/min, which is 16 above rest and well below the 150 at minute 0.

Challenge the claim

“When the rally gets longer, the heart makes more oxygen for the player’s legs.”

On your board Replace this claim with two sentences. Use the oxygen route to explain where oxygen comes from. Use the glucose route to explain the heart’s role in delivering both materials.

Score the explanation

  1. Oxygen comes from inhaled air and crosses from lungs into blood.
  2. The heart pumps blood carrying oxygen and absorbed glucose to the muscles. Muscle cells use both to release energy.
