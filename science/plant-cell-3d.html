Nine parts, different jobs

Cell wall
Rigid outer layer that supports the cell; made of cellulose.
Cell membrane
Thin, flexible layer that controls what moves in and out.
Cytoplasm
Jelly-like fluid, mostly water, between the membrane and nucleus.
Vacuole
Stores water, nutrients and wastes. A large vacuole also helps hold the plant cell’s shape.
Nucleus
The control centre; contains DNA, the instructions for the cell.
Chloroplast
Where photosynthesis happens; contains the green pigment chlorophyll.
Mitochondria
Turn sugar into usable energy.
Endoplasmic reticulum
Folded membranes next to the nucleus that build proteins and move materials.
Golgi apparatus
Packages and ships materials; sits on its own, away from the nucleus.
