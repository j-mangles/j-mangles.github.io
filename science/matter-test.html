Matter at the Test Bench

Individual test · Grade 8 Science · 20 marks

Use the measurements and observations below. Show calculations with units. You may use a calculator.

The submerged sample

A uniform solid has a mass of 64.8 g. It is completely submerged without spilling water or trapping bubbles. The water reading rises from 38 mL to 65 mL. Fresh water has a density of 1.00 g/mL.

Two bottles on a ramp

Each trial uses a fresh 4 mL drop on the same clean ramp at 20 °C. Each drop travels 15 cm.

Air and water in syringes

Two capped syringes each start at 14 mL. A student pushes equally hard on each plunger. The air volume drops noticeably. The water volume barely changes.

An ice sample warms

A heater supplies energy steadily to pure ice at ordinary air pressure. In interval A the temperature rises from −6 °C to 0 °C. In interval B, ice and liquid coexist at 0 °C.

A lake in winter

A lake develops a layer of ice at its surface. Liquid water remains beneath that layer.
