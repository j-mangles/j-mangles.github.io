Which sample is aluminium?

The labels fell off two solid samples. One is dense plastic and one is aluminium. Choose tests, collect readings and identify each sample. Swap who measures and who calculates for the second sample.

Identify a fresh sample

Work independently. Use the readings to find the sample’s volume, calculate its density and identify its material. Each part earns one mark on your first submission.
