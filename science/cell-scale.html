Cell Scale Explorer

Cells vary in size. Many bacteria are about 0.1–5 micrometres (μm) across. Typical plant and animal cells are about 10–100 μm across. Zoom the scale below and select a range to explore it.

Compare the size ranges

This is a logarithmic scale: each labelled step is 10 times larger than the one before it. The bands show approximate ranges, not the size of every cell.

Keep the units straight

One centimetre equals 10,000 μm. A micrometre is one millionth of a metre. Cell diagrams in your notes are enlarged so the parts are visible; their drawings are not all to the same scale.

These size ranges are approximate. Cells in the same group can differ, and diagrams are models that help us compare—not photographs of every cell.
