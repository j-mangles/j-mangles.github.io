Headline Framing Lab

The same facts can produce very different headlines and images. Experiment with emphasis, check what's missing, and practise verifying a claim before sharing it.

All events, headlines and images on this page are invented for practice. Nothing here reports an actual school decision. This is not a graded assessment; your writing stays in this tab until you choose to copy it.
