The missing stone shipment.

A boat carrying fine limestone was expected at the northern landing. The builders say it never arrived. Your job isn't to guess who is guilty—it's to find out what really happened.

A living world, a fictional mystery. Walk along the river, inspect physical traces, speak with a scribe and hauling crew, and reach a conclusion using evidence. The reconstructed landscape and characters are interpretations, not a photograph of the past.
