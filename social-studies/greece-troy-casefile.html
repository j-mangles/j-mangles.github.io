Evidence cards

Homeric poems

The stories developed centuries after the events they describe and drew on oral traditions.

Can show: stories people kept and retold.

Hisarlik layers

Excavations revealed many cities built one above another at the place associated with Troy.

Can show: a real settlement existed and changed over time.

Schliemann’s treasure

The famous gold treasure was much older than the possible Late Bronze Age conflict.

Limit: the label “Priam’s Treasure” did not make it proof of Homer’s story.

Late Bronze Age layer

One Troy layer was destroyed around 1180 BCE. Archaeology can examine the destruction, but it cannot identify every character in a poem.
