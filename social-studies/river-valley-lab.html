Interactive historical reasoning · Cluster 2

River Valley Decision Lab

You're advising a community along an ancient river. Each decision has possible benefits and costs. Use historical context, consider whose interests are affected, and explain why you chose your response.

This is a learning scenario, not a historical simulation of an actual city. The decisions and consequences are illustrative, not quotations or precise reconstructions. No answers are sent or saved online.

Choose a setting

How historians think

  • Geography: What opportunities and risks does the river create?
  • Technology: What can canals, tools and records accomplish?
  • Society: Who benefits, who works, and who decides?
  • Evidence: What source would you need to test your claim?
Curriculum connections

Manitoba Grade 8: 8-KL-023/024, 8-KI-013, 8-KP-045, 8-KE-054, 8-S-301/302/305/306.

Teacher pacing: 5 minutes of introduction, 18 minutes of decisions, 12 minutes of pair discussion, 10 minutes of evidence reflection and exit slip.

Exit slip: Which decision seemed most reasonable, and what information would make you change your mind?
