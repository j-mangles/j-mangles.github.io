Clues from the lesson

Myth

A traditional story could explain nature or human experience and communicate values. The Demeter and Persephone story connected to seasonal change.

Oracle

At Delphi, the Pythia gave answers associated with Apollo. People and states sought guidance there.

Olympic festival

The festival at Olympia honoured Zeus. It combined religion, athletic competition and rivalry between poleis.
