03 · SOURCE READING

What is “The Satire of the Trades” evidence of?

Khety describes other jobs to his son Pepi while taking him by boat to scribal school. The student assignment paraphrases most descriptions; its final line is quoted. Student scribes copied the text for handwriting practice.

What the text says

The potter is muddier than a pig. The washerman works beside crocodiles. The bricklayer is covered in filth. Then Khety praises the scribe’s position.

Who benefits from this message?

Khety wants his son to value scribal education. The text may tell us more about scribes’ views than about every worker’s daily life.
