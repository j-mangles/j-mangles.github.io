Delian League Hearing

Examine how an alliance against Persia became an Athenian empire. The same evidence can look different to a city leading the league and to a city paying tribute.

Evidence on the table

Shared defense

The Delian League began as an alliance formed after the Persian Wars.

Ships or tribute

Members contributed ships or money. Many paid tribute, giving Athens greater control over resources.

Treasury moved

In 454 BCE the treasury moved from Delos to Athens.

Leaving was restricted

Athens limited members’ freedom to leave; cities that tried were forced back.

The Parthenon

Athens built a major sanctuary to Athena on the Acropolis between 447 and 432 BCE. Admiring the building does not settle who paid or whether the system was fair.

Check the change

1. What was the league’s original purpose?
2. Which evidence most strongly suggests Athens had turned the alliance into an empire?
3. Does the Parthenon’s beauty alone prove the tribute system was fair?
