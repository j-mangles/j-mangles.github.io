Three evidence cards

Founding legend

Romans told a story that Romulus founded the city in 753 BCE after a conflict with his twin Remus.

Can help show: how Romans explained their origins and identity.

Livy’s account

Livy wrote a history of Rome centuries after the traditional founding and noted that early stories were difficult to verify.

Limit: later writing is not a witness to the founding.

Palatine remains

Archaeologists have found evidence of early settlement on Rome’s Palatine Hill.

Can support: people lived there in antiquity. It does not identify Romulus.
