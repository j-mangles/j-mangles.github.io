Grade 8 Social Studies · Ancient Greece · Lesson 13

Greece in Review Circuit

Travel through four short stations to retrieve the unit’s big ideas: geography, evidence, civic life and historical order. Try from memory first, then use the lesson notes to check your thinking.

This is low-stakes practice. Your choices and writing stay on this page and disappear when you leave or clear them.

Station 1 · Place and connection

Build the map idea

Station 2 · Evidence and uncertainty

Sort the Troy claims

Choose the best evidence-based reading of the archaeological record.

Station 3 · Rules and lived experience

Ask “for whom?”

Who could normally vote in the Athenian Assembly?
Which statement best describes Spartan society?

Station 4 · Sequence and consequence

Put events in order

Give each event its position from 1 (earliest) to 4 (latest). BCE dates count down as time moves forward.

Make a connection

What did Greece leave, and who paid?

Choose one idea you want to remember and connect it to evidence from at least two lessons. Include a person or group whose experience was different from the most powerful citizens.
