Whose Golden Age? A Walk Through Athens

Grade 8 Social Studies · Ancient Greece · Lessons 5, 8 and 10

Whose golden age was it?

Walk to five places in and around Athens. At each one, look at the evidence, sort what it can and can't tell you, and pin one card to your notebook. At the end you answer the question in a paragraph.

This activity discusses slavery and violence.

Nothing is saved. If you close or reload the page, your work is gone, so copy or download your answer before you leave.
