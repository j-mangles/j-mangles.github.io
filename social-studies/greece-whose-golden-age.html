Whose Golden Age? A Walk Through Athens

Grade 8 Social Studies · Ancient Greece · Lessons 5, 8 and 10

Whose golden age was it?

You are on a dig. Walk through Athens, find the survey markers at five places, and work out what each piece of evidence can and can't tell you. At the end you answer the question in a paragraph.

The dig supervisor who gives you your tasks is an invented, present-day character. No one from ancient Athens speaks in this game, because the game only uses evidence that survives.

This activity discusses slavery and violence.

Nothing is saved. If you close or reload the page, your work is gone, so copy or download your answer before you leave.
