Pharaoh Report Cards

Back to the Egypt atlas

What can one ruler actually change about a civilization? Use the four jobs of a pharaoh to make your own report cards, check the evidence behind three famous claims, and decide which changes lasted. Grades are your reasoned judgment, not an answer key.

Station 1 · Make a judgment

How well did each pharaoh do the job?

The four jobs are keeping order (ma’at), defending Egypt, building for the gods, and providing for the people. Choose an evidence-based rating, or mark “?” if you need more evidence. Nothing is submitted or saved online.

Station 2 · Read the source

Fact or spin?

Choose a verdict, then check who benefits from telling the story that way. Try all three claims before moving on.

Station 3 · Explain your judgment

Who changed Egypt the most?

Choose one pharaoh, use two specific pieces of evidence, and decide whether the change lasted. More than one answer can be defended.

Your evidence-based claim

Check your reasoning
  • Did you distinguish what the pharaoh did from what later writers or monuments claimed?
  • Did you explain whether the change lasted after the ruler died?
  • Did you use two accurate details and acknowledge a limit in what the sources tell us?
  • Could another pharaoh be a reasonable choice for a different reason?

There is no single winner. Akhenaten attempted a sweeping change that did not last; Ramesses II left many monuments and a peace treaty; Cleopatra’s defeat ended Egypt’s independence; Hatshepsut showed a woman could rule as pharaoh. Your evidence and explanation matter more than the name you choose.
