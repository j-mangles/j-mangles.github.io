Slavery in Rome: Source Lens

Study what a law or object can reveal about Roman slavery, and what it cannot tell us about an individual's thoughts or full experience. Do not treat a person as a role-play character; focus on evidence, power and human agency.

Roman law

Roman law treated enslaved people as property, while lived conditions varied by work and place.

An inscribed collar or other object

Material evidence can document control or ownership. It does not preserve every person's voice.

Look for agency as well as control

The lesson notes describe escape and resistance alongside many kinds of forced work. Use that evidence without assuming every person had the same experience.
