Egypt on Exhibit

Grade 8 Social Studies · Individual source test · 12 marks

You are the museum editor. Read the three draft captions, then revise each one using the exhibit file.

The exhibit file

These source notes paraphrase records discussed in class. Each proposed caption makes a claim that goes beyond its source.

1. Unification

Draft caption “The palette gives us an exact account of how Narmer conquered both lands.”

A · Narmer Palette
One side shows Narmer wearing the White Crown of Upper Egypt. On the other, he wears the Red Crown of Lower Egypt. A royal victory scene appears on the palette.

2. Workers at Giza

Draft caption “Finding a workers’ town proves every pyramid worker freely chose the job.”

B · Town and cemetery at Giza
Archaeologists found workers’ living quarters and a cemetery beside the pyramid site. The class evidence describes crews that were housed and fed.

3. Power at Deir el-Medina

Draft caption “Because the tomb builders received their grain, every Egyptian worker could make officials pay on demand.”

C · Turin Strike Papyrus
The royal tomb builders’ ration was 18 days late. They left work. After a partial payment they demanded to see the vizier. They eventually received what they were owed.
