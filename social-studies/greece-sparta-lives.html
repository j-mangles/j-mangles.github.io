Three Lives in Sparta

Compare a Spartiate, a perioikos and a helot. Sparta’s military system depended on unequal rights and compelled labour, so ask who benefited and who carried the cost.

Spartiates

Full male citizens devoted substantial time to military obligations. Boys entered the state-organized agoge.

Perioikoi

Free non-citizens in nearby towns who traded and produced goods under Spartan rule.

Helots

Unfree agricultural workers from conquered communities. Their labour supported Sparta; rebellions and the much larger helot population shaped Spartan fears.
