03 · THINK LIKE A HISTORIAN

Was Menes Narmer?

Later king lists call the first pharaoh “Menes.” Historians debate whether this was Narmer or his successor Hor-Aha. Pick one source and decide what it can—and cannot—show.

The Narmer Palette

Shows Narmer in both the White Crown and the Red Crown, and shows a “smiting” scene. Royal art could glorify its ruler.

Seal impressions at Abydos

List Narmer as the first king of the first dynasty. This supports Narmer as the first ruler, but does not directly explain the later name Menes.

A 2014 Egyptologist survey

41 surveyed scholars favored Menes = Narmer; 31 favored Menes = Hor-Aha. A poll shows a live debate, not proof of either answer.
